> [!TIP]
> **Idea 1 (Director's Cut)** transforms developer projects into cinematic film clips. Users can scrub through a 3D editing room timeline, inspect 3D film slates, and view director's commentary on technical architectural decisions.

---

## 🌟 Key Components

- 📽️ `FilmSlate3D.tsx`: Interactive 3D film clapboard rendering project metadata.
- 🎚️ `TimelineScrubber.tsx`: Frame-by-frame career scrubber navigating milestones.
- 🎞️ `EditingRoomScene.tsx`: 3D room environment with lighting and camera controls.
- 🎬 `DirectorsCutModal.tsx`: Detailed breakdown of engineering challenges and key code metrics.
